# Part 2 — Embeddings

> Part 2 of **[Tokens to Agents](../../README.md)** — the foundations of agentic AI, built from scratch.

**From counting words to placing them in space.**

N-grams treat "excellent" and "superb" as unrelated symbols. Embeddings fix that by learning geometry from co-occurrence — the first step toward meaning.

Covers: skip-gram Word2Vec with negative sampling (gradients derived and implemented by hand), PPMI co-occurrence matrices, TF-IDF as a baseline, and cosine similarity for evaluating what was learned.
