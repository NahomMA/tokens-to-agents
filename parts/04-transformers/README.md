# Part 4 — Transformers

> Part 4 of **[Tokens to Agents](../../README.md)** — the foundations of agentic AI, built from scratch.

**Build the architecture, then look inside a real one.**

Two halves. First, an encoder and a decoder written from scratch — positional encoding, scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, residuals and normalisation. Then the same machinery observed in Llama-3-8B, with the attention weights pulled out and mapped.

Covers: the from-scratch build and its classification results; attention interpretability on a production model, including attention sinks, positional heads, and syntactic-dependency heads — plus why attention is not an explanation.
